    Staff Selection Commission (SSC) CHSL Exam 2026

    Post Update: 15 July 2026 | Short Details of Information

    Staff Selection Commission (SSC) has released the Application Status and Admit Card for the Combined Higher Secondary Level (CHSL) 10+2 Tier 1 Examination 2026. Candidates who have applied for the LDC / JSA / DEO vacancies can now download their hall tickets.

    Candidates must carry a printed copy of the Admit Card along with original photo ID proof and passport size photographs to the examination center on the allotted date and shift.

    How to Download Admit Card & Exam Instructions 2026
    • Staff Selection Commission (SSC) CHSL Admit Card 2026 is released Region Wise. Candidates can download their admit card from their respective regional website links below.
    • To download the Admit Card or check Application Status, you will need your Registration Number and Date of Birth (DOB) or Candidate's Name & Father's Name.
    • The final admit card with the exam center details will be available to download exactly 4 days before your exam date.
    • Take a clear printout (Color or Black & White) of the Admit Card on A4 size paper.
    • Mandatory at Exam Center: Candidates MUST carry the printed Admit Card, 2 Passport Size Photos, and Original Valid Photo ID Proof (Aadhar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Driving License).
    • Ensure that the Date of Birth on your ID Proof matches exactly with the Admit Card. If not, carry the original 10th marksheet as proof of DOB.
    Important Note: All the information given in this post is in short. Please strictly follow the reporting time and dress code instructions printed on your Admit Card. Entry will be strictly closed after the gate closing time.

    Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

    Q. When can I download the final Admit Card?

    Ans: You can download your final admit card exactly 4 days prior to your designated exam date.

    Q. What documents do I need to carry to the exam center?

    Ans: You must carry a printed copy of your admit card, two passport size color photographs, and an original valid photo ID proof (like Aadhar or PAN).

    Q. I forgot my Registration Number. How can I download the admit card?

    Ans: You can search and download your admit card using your First Name, Father's Name, and Date of Birth on the SSC regional website.
