    UPTET 2026 Official Answer Key Released

    Uttar Pradesh Basic Education Board (UPBEB) Exam 2026

    Post Update: 15 July 2026 | Short Details of Information

    Uttar Pradesh Basic Education Board (UPBEB) has successfully conducted the UP Teacher Eligibility Test (UPTET) 2026. Candidates who appeared in the examination can now download the Official Answer Key for both Paper I (Primary) and Paper II (Upper Primary).

    If any candidate finds any discrepancy or wrong answer in the official answer key, they can submit an online objection before the last date by paying the required objection fee per question.

    Important Dates & Objection Fee
    • UPTET Exam Date : 10/07/2026
    • Answer Key Available : 15/07/2026
    • Last Date for File Objection : 20/07/2026
    • Final Answer Key : Notified Soon
    • UPTET Result Declared : Notified Soon
    • Objection Fee (Per Question) : ₹500/-
    • General / OBC / SC / ST : Same Fee Applicable
    • Pay the Objection Fee through Debit Card, Credit Card, Net Banking.
    • Note: If the objection is found correct, the fee will be refunded.

    How to Download / Check Answer Key 2026
    • Uttar Pradesh Basic Education Board (UPBEB) UPTET Exam 2026 Answer Key is Released. Candidates can check their answers within the given dates.
    • Click on the "Download Answer Key" link provided in the Important Links section below.
    • Download the PDF file for your respective Paper and match your answers according to your Booklet Series (Set A, B, C, D).
    • If you find any discrepancy or incorrect answer given by the board, you can file an objection through the official portal.
    • To file an objection, candidates need to log in using their Registration Number, Password, and Captcha code.
    • Take a printout of the answer key and the objection payment receipt for future reference.
    Important Note: All the information given in this post is in short. Candidates are advised to read the official notice before filing any objections regarding the Answer Key.
