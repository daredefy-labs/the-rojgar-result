    Post Update: 15 July 2026 | Detailed Syllabus Information

    Staff Selection Commission (SSC) has released the revised Exam Pattern and Syllabus for the Combined Graduate Level (CGL) Examination 2026. Candidates who have applied for the upcoming SSC CGL Tier-I exam must start their preparation strictly according to the latest official syllabus.

    The selection process consists of Tier-I and Tier-II Computer Based Examinations. Below is the detailed subject-wise weightage and a direct link to download the complete PDF.

    Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

    Q. Is there any negative marking in SSC CGL Exam?

    Ans: Yes, there is a negative marking of 0.50 marks for every incorrect answer in the Tier-I examination.

    Q. What is the total time duration for the Tier-1 Exam?

    Ans: The total time duration allowed is 60 minutes (1 Hour) for 100 questions.
