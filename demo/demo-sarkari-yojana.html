    PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana 2026 Online Form

    Government of India - National Rooftop Solar Portal

    Post Update: 15 July 2026 | Short Details of Scheme

    The Government of India has launched the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana 2026 to provide up to 300 units of free electricity per month to poor and middle-class families. Under this scheme, subsidies will be provided directly to the bank accounts of the beneficiaries for installing solar panels on their rooftops.

    Eligible citizens of India can apply online through the official National Rooftop Solar Portal. Read the complete eligibility criteria and required documents list before applying.

    Scheme Eligibility & Main Benefits
    Who Can Apply? (Eligibility) Benefits of the Scheme
    • Applicant must be an Indian citizen.
    • Must own a house with a roof suitable for installing solar panels.
    • Must have a valid electricity connection in their own name.
    • Applicant must not have availed any other subsidy for solar panels previously.
    • Free Electricity: Up to 300 units of free electricity every month.
    • Subsidy: Up to ₹78,000 subsidy deposited directly into the bank account (for a 3kW system).
    • Extra income by selling surplus electricity to DISCOMs.
    List of Required Documents (ज़रूरी कागज़ात)
    • Aadhar Card (Linked with Mobile No.)
    • Recent Electricity Bill (Latest)
    • Electricity Consumer Number
    • Bank Passbook (For subsidy transfer)
    • Income Certificate / Ration Card
    • Passport Size Photograph
    How to Apply Online / Fill Registration Form
    • Candidate Can Apply for PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana through the official portal.
    • Step 1: Go to "Apply Online" link given below and Register with your State, Electricity Distribution Company, and Electricity Consumer Number.
    • Step 2: Log in with your Consumer Number and Mobile Number. Apply for the Rooftop Solar as per the given form.
    • Kindly Check and Collect All Documents – Aadhar, Electricity Bill, Bank Passbook details.
    • Kindly Ready Scan Document Related to Scheme Form – Photo, Passbook, Bill, Etc. (Photo Size 16x5 cm recommended as per standard form sizes).
    • Once the DISCOM grants feasibility approval, get the plant installed by a registered vendor.
    • After installation, submit plant details and apply for a net meter. Finally, provide bank details on the portal to receive your subsidy.
    Important Note: The Government of India never asks for any OTP or money to transfer subsidies. Always apply through the official PM Surya Ghar portal. Read complete guidelines before registering.

    Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

    Q. Who is eligible for PM Surya Ghar Yojana?

    Ans: Any Indian citizen owning a house with a valid electricity connection in their name is eligible.

    Q. Is there any fee to apply for this scheme?

    Ans: No, the online registration for this scheme is completely free of cost.

    Q. How will I receive the subsidy amount?

    Ans: The subsidy amount will be transferred directly to the beneficiary's provided bank account via DBT within 30 days of successful installation.
